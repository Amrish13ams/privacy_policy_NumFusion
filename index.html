Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 20, 2024

NumFusion ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you play NumFusion (Package Name: com.mcsakar.numfusion).

1. Information Collection & Usage

  • Local Gameplay Data: NumFusion stores your game progress, current score, best score, undo history, and settings (such as sound, vibration, and dark mode preferences) locally on your device using Android DataStore. We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal gameplay data on any external servers.
  • Internet Access: The app requires internet access solely to load and display advertisements via third-party advertising networks.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to display banner and full-screen interstitial advertisements in the app.

  • Google AdMob may collect and use certain data (such as device identifiers, IP addresses, and ad interaction data) to provide personalized or non-personalized ads in accordance with Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • You can manage your ad personalization settings through your device's Google account settings.

3. Children's Privacy

NumFusion is designed as a casual puzzle game suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
